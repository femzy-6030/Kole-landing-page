Why Traditional Waste Collection Fails Homes

We compared old neighborhood waste habits with the on-demand KóLẹ̀ platform experience.

The Old Way

Unpredictable & Unhygienic

  • ✕ Irregular Pickup Trucks: Waiting weeks for municipal trucks leads to overflowing bins and bad odors.
  • ✕ Illegal Roadside Dumping: Uncollected trash ends up burned in compounds or dumped in gutters during rainfall.
  • ✕ Unclear Bargaining Fees: Informal cart pushers charge unpredictable prices without receipts.
Result: Odors, health hazards, and cluttered compounds.
The KóLẹ̀ Way

On-Demand & Verified

  • ✓ Instant Dispatch: Request a pickup on your schedule—a verified collector arrives within 24 hours.
  • ✓ Flat Pricing: Simple standard household rate per pickup with digital payment verification.
  • ✓ Government-Approved Dumpsites: 100% responsible disposal to keep Osogbo clean and sustainable.
Result: Clean compounds, scheduled pickups, and peace of mind.
